How it works .

Chess steganography: mathematically concealing a message inside a perfectly legal game. Everything below is how the numbers behave nothing here ever leaves your browser.

♟

The PGN channel

1. e4 · the text

Text becomes binary. At every position, the legal moves are sorted alphabetically a deterministic order. If N moves are legal, the position carries ⌊log₂ N⌋ bits: just enough to choose one specific move among the valid ones. The result is a game that looks natural development, captures, castling and plays on any PGN viewer.

♛

The FEN key

position · the password

A random, fully legal position serves as a key you can hold visually. Two people who independently arrive at the same position in this app, in Lichess, from a printed diagram always derive the exact same key. Only the piece placement matters: whose move it is, castling rights and clocks are invisible on a static picture, so they are ignored when the key is hashed.

◆

The key field

one field · three cases

There is no mode switch to remember. The key field decides: blank means public anyone can decode; a plain password encrypts the payload; a pasted FEN position is recognized and hashed from its placement. The same field, checked the same way on both pages, so nothing can drift out of sync.

◇

The decoy

cover · never an error

A wrong or missing key never errors out it returns plausible chess commentary, seeded deterministically from the game and the attempt, so the same mistake always gets the same polite cover. There is no way to tell a decoy from a genuine empty reading except by holding the right key.

The promise

on your side of the screen

Everything composing, replaying, hashing, decrypting runs inside your browser. No account, no server, no logs. The only round trip in the system is the one you choose to make: taking a game out into the world and back.

i-crypt all processing runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your machine.
